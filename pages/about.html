Our story, mission, and the vision behind Veritas AI Solutions.

Our Story

Veritas AI Solutions was born from a simple conviction: the best education combines timeless truth with cutting-edge innovation.

Our founder, Arun Pereira, has spent over 20 years in the trenches of Christian education — from founding Gospel Haiti International School to leading school improvement initiatives using the Baldrige Performance Excellence Framework. When AI began transforming education, he saw both a challenge and an opportunity.

The challenge: Christian schools and families were being left behind, unsure how to adopt technology that seemed to conflict with their values.

The opportunity: to build something better — AI-powered education that strengthens rather than undermines a biblical worldview.

That's why we built The Gospel Academy. And that's why we consult with schools across the country, helping them navigate the AI revolution with wisdom and confidence.

Our Mission

To transform Christian education through the integration of artificial intelligence, proven organizational excellence frameworks, and a commitment to biblical truth — making world-class education accessible to families and schools worldwide.

Meet Our Founder

Arun Pereira

Founder, Veritas AI Solutions

Arun Pereira is the founder of Veritas AI Solutions and The Gospel Academy, an AI-powered Christian education platform serving Pre-K through Grade 12.

With over 20 years of leadership in international education — including founding Gospel Haiti International School — and 15 years applying the Baldrige Performance Excellence Framework to school improvement, Arun brings a rare combination of operational rigor, technological innovation, and missional focus to the transformation of education.

He holds deep expertise in AI implementation, curriculum development, and building sustainable educational programs in challenging international contexts.
